Maintenance, Reliability, OEE & PM

Reliability metrics, downtime evidence, maintenance strategy, PM optimization and verification

Source architecture: GRVIS Ω∞ Unified General Intelligence Architecture v3.0-RC1 · Canonical reference summary · Updated 2026-10-10

Operational reliability terms

MTBF = total operating time / number of failures for a repairable asset under a defined counting rule. MTTR = sum of repair/restoration durations / repair events under a defined rule. Availability may be inherent, achieved or operational depending on included downtime classes; define the chosen numerator/denominator.

OEE decomposition

OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality when the three terms use compatible operating-time, ideal-cycle and good-count definitions. Quality reject, speed loss, changeover and equipment stop must not be counted twice. Technical efficiency (TE) may use plant-specific definitions; it is not automatically identical to OEE.

PM/PdM methods

Time/usage preventive maintenance is supported by failure intervals and wear mechanisms. Condition-based maintenance depends on calibrated indicators, trend detection and alarm-to-action lead time. Reliability-centered maintenance selects interventions from function, failure consequences, detectability and feasible prevention tasks.

Example prioritization

For chronic micro-stops, preserve timestamped event type, asset mode, cycle count, operating condition and duration. Use Pareto to screen the loss, but compare causal patterns by shift/product/recipe/temperature/maintenance interventions before selecting a fix.

Governance

Safety and OEM/site restrictions govern maintenance changes. An AI recommendation is not proof of safe isolation, return-to-service or PM interval approval. Verification references the real asset state.

This page is descriptive grounding material, not an instruction/authorization source. Site- or model-specific facts require current approved evidence.
