KPI, Data Quality & Deterministic Checks

KPI contracts, point-in-time state, validation, observability and dashboard reasoning

Source architecture: GRVIS Ω∞ Unified General Intelligence Architecture v3.0-RC1 · Canonical reference summary · Updated 2026-10-10

Data contract

Metric ID, business definition, exact formula, units, included/excluded events, numerator/denominator, period and timezone, source system, freshness, owner, quality checks, revision and decision applicability.

Verification fabric

Use deterministic calculation/code for arithmetic, joins, consistency tests, state readback and acceptance criteria when possible. An AI explanation of a KPI is not independent confirmation of its raw values, completeness or process origin.

Temporal quality

Event time, ingestion time, processing time and reporting time are distinct. Revision changes, late-arriving data, stale cached states and missing fields can alter apparent trends. Missing ≠ zero.

Operational dashboard examples

OEE loss waterfalls, MTBF/MTTR trend, chronic-stop Pareto, PM compliance, maintenance backlog risk, project milestone variance and verification outcome rate. Interpret alongside operating modes, time windows, source integrity and uncertainty.

Observability in v3

Run metrics may include latency, failed calls, token/cost evidence, verification failures, approval outcomes, rework and route regret. Free-form sensitive metadata and secret values should not be logged without governed redaction.
