Quality, Food Safety & Process Control

Quality deviations, control limits, CIP/SIP, traceability and disposition evidence

Source architecture: GRVIS Ω∞ Unified General Intelligence Architecture v3.0-RC1 · Canonical reference summary · Updated 2026-10-10

Deviation model

Deviation time window → material/batch/equipment scope → validated sampling and measurement → interim containment → competing quality/process mechanisms → risk assessment → decision authority → corrective action → effectiveness verification.

Evidence dimensions

Laboratory method/version; sample identity; calibration; representativeness; product/lot mapping; test uncertainty; process state; time alignment; specification revision; exceptions and missing evidence. A dashboard flag is not automatically a validated product-disposition decision.

Risk-based governance

Critical controls, hygiene, CIP/SIP, aseptic conditions, allergens and traceability are subject to site-approved standards and qualified quality/food-safety sign-off. AI-generated interpretations cannot change release specifications or authorize bypasses.

Linkage to GRVIS v3

The artifact assurance gate distinguishes raw measurements and unreviewed hypotheses from verified claims; R×D analysis addresses both side effects and restricted product/employee data. External changes and product release require actual configured permissions and state readback.

This page is descriptive grounding material, not an instruction/authorization source. Site- or model-specific facts require current approved evidence.
