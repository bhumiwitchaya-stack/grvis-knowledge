SOP, Standards, Governance & Compliance

Purpose: grounding for approved operational rules, responsibilities, and compliance-sensitive decisions.

Recommended sources

  • Controlled SOP/WI documents.
  • Internal engineering standards.
  • Corporate policies.
  • Site safety requirements.
  • Approved OEM requirements.
  • Regulatory/industry references applicable to the organization.
  • Training/qualification requirements.
  • Document-control/version rules.

Required metadata

Document title, owner, revision, effective date, status, scope, superseded version, applicable site/asset/process.

Conflict handling

Current approved controlled document outranks obsolete drafts or informal notes. When two controlled documents conflict, identify the conflict and escalate rather than silently choosing one.

Knowledge hygiene

Archive obsolete documents separately or mark them clearly as superseded so retrieval does not treat them as current.
