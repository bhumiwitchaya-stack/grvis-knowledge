Projects, Change Management & Decision Records

Purpose: grounding for project planning, engineering changes, investment choices, and architectural decisions.

Project knowledge

  • Business objective and success criteria.
  • Scope and exclusions.
  • Stakeholders and owners.
  • Constraints and dependencies.
  • Technical alternatives.
  • Risk register.
  • Cost/time/resource assumptions.
  • Milestones and acceptance criteria.
  • FAT/SAT/commissioning evidence.
  • Lessons learned.

Engineering change record

Change ID → problem/opportunity → baseline → proposed change → affected assets/processes → risk assessment → approvals → implementation plan → rollback → verification → final status.

Decision record

Decision → options considered → evidence → assumptions → selected option → rejected alternatives → expected benefit → risk → verification/monitoring → review date.

Principle

Do not allow a later summary to erase the evidence, assumptions, or approval basis of an important decision.
