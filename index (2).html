Data, KPI & Dashboard Definitions

Purpose: grounding for consistent calculations, dashboards, reports, and operational decisions.

Store here

  • KPI definitions and formulas.
  • Source systems/tables/files.
  • Data owners.
  • Sampling/update frequency.
  • Time-zone and reporting-period rules.
  • Missing-data handling.
  • Planned/unplanned downtime classification.
  • Production-state definitions.
  • Units and conversion rules.
  • Dashboard field dictionary.
  • Threshold/target definitions.
  • Data-quality checks.

KPI contract

KPI name → business meaning → exact formula → numerator/denominator → inclusions/exclusions → source → refresh interval → owner → target → caveats.

Data-quality rule

A dashboard number is not decision-grade unless its definition, source, time window, and completeness are known.

Change control

Formula changes require revision history because otherwise trend comparisons can become invalid.
