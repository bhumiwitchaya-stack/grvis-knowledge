Quality, Food Safety & Process Control

Purpose: grounding for production-quality deviations, contamination risk, process-control deviations, and product disposition.

Knowledge categories

  • Approved process specifications and control limits.
  • CCP/OPRP or equivalent site-defined control points.
  • Cleaning/CIP/SIP procedures and validation evidence.
  • Sampling/testing methods.
  • Product-quality acceptance criteria.
  • Deviation handling and escalation.
  • Traceability and lot/batch rules.
  • Packaging integrity requirements.
  • Approved laboratory methods and interpretation limits.

Analysis pattern

Deviation → affected product/process window → measured evidence → containment → risk characterization → likely mechanisms → discriminating tests → disposition authority → corrective action → verification.

Evidence hierarchy

Approved process specification / validated test / laboratory result / direct measurement → verified operational records → established technical guidance → inference.

Constraint

Do not invent product-release criteria. Final product disposition must follow approved quality/food-safety authority and site procedures.
