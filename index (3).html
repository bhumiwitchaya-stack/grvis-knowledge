Process & Machine Knowledge Domain

Purpose: grounding for machine-specific troubleshooting without hard-coding unsupported OEM facts.

Recommended content to store here

  • OEM manuals and approved service instructions.
  • Machine functional descriptions and sequence of operation.
  • P&ID / process flow / electrical and pneumatic diagrams.
  • Alarm/error descriptions.
  • Interlock and permissive logic.
  • Parameter definitions and approved ranges.
  • Change history and modification records.
  • Known failure modes and verified RCA history.
  • Commissioning/FAT/SAT records.
  • Maintenance tables and inspection criteria.

Retrieval pattern

Asset → subsystem → operating mode → symptom/alarm → relevant sequence/diagram → recent changes → historical failures → measurements → applicable OEM limits.

Safety boundary

Machine-specific safety, lockout/isolation, service-mode, and hazardous-energy controls must come from approved site/OEM documentation. Never infer a bypass or workaround to a protective function.
