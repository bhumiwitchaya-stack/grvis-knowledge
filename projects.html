Projects, CAPEX, Change & FAT/SAT

Engineering project decisions, EEM, MOC, commissioning, project risks and verification

Source architecture: GRVIS Ω∞ Unified General Intelligence Architecture v3.0-RC1 · Canonical reference summary · Updated 2026-10-10

Lifecycle

Business case → URS → architecture/options → risk and ROI assumptions → design review → procurement → FAT → installation → SAT/commissioning → reliability ramp-up → training/handover → benefit realization.

Decision record

Decision ID, objective, baseline, options, evidence, assumptions, constraints, affected parties, risk map, cost/time/benefit, selected option, approver, verification plan, rollback and post-implementation outcome.

Change management

Management of Change (MOC) covers equipment/process/safety/quality/automation changes. Review scope, drawings, stakeholder approvals, training, spares, maintenance readiness, lockout/isolation implications and updated controlled documentation before operational handover.

Early Equipment Management

Design-for-reliability and maintainability starts before installation. Capture failure/access/cleaning/inspection/testability concerns in URS, FAT/SAT acceptance and post-startup technical efficiency metrics.

v3 durable action

External changes can be ambiguous after network or tool failure. GRVIS v3 describes OUTCOME_UNKNOWN, idempotency, checkpoint/resume and external-state reconciliation rather than automatically repeating a potentially duplicate action.
